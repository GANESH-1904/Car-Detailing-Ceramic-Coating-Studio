THE SCIENCE

WHAT LIES BENEATH

Know your paint,
protect your paint.

Why we measure first and polish second.

The layers we protect

Modern paint is thinner than a sheet of paper. Over-polishing removes clear coat that can never be replaced.

  • Ceramic coating: a hard sacrificial layer that takes the daily abuse
  • Clear coat: typically 40–60 microns, measured before every correction
  • Base colour and primer: kept sealed from UV and moisture
  • Panel gauge readings are recorded in your inspection report

ASK THE FOUNDER

COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers
from the bay.

Four things owners ask us most.

Is coating worth it?

For a car you keep more than two years and park outside, yes. It makes washing easier and slows swirl damage. It is not a force field.

How often should I polish?

As rarely as possible. Each polish removes clear coat, so we correct only when defects are visible, and protect afterwards.

Can you fix deep scratches?

If a fingernail catches, it is through the clear coat. We can reduce it, but repainting may be the honest answer. We tell you at inspection.

Do you handle older cars?

Yes. Older single-stage paint is thin, so we use gentle polishes and measure often.

GOOD TO KNOW

FAQS & SUPPORT

Before you
book.

How long does a treatment take?

An interior atelier session usually takes half a day, paint correction one to two days, and a full ceramic coating two to three days including curing time. We confirm the exact timeline after inspecting your vehicle.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. The studio works by appointment only, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Use the booking form below and the team will confirm a slot.

What does paint correction involve?

Paint is washed and decontaminated, then machine-polished in stages to remove swirls and light scratches, and finally refined for depth and gloss.

How do I look after a ceramic coating?

Wash with a pH-neutral shampoo using the two-bucket method, avoid automatic brush washes, and book a yearly maintenance check to keep the hydrophobic effect strong.
