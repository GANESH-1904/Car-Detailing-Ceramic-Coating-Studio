    DIY GUIDES

    CARE BETWEEN VISITS

    Do it right
    at home.

    Short guides for the four jobs owners get wrong most.

    Washing

    • Rinse first to lift loose dust
    • Two buckets: one soapy, one clean
    • Wash top to bottom with a soft mitt

    Drying

    • Use a large plush towel
    • Blot or pull gently, never rub
    • Dry door jambs too

    Glass

    • Use a streak-free glass cleaner
    • Two towels: wipe then buff
    • Clean wipers to avoid smears

    Interior

    • Vacuum before wiping
    • Use a soft brush on vents
    • Condition leather every few months

    GOOD TO KNOW

    FAQS & SUPPORT

    Before you
    book.

    How long does a treatment take?

    An interior atelier session usually takes half a day, paint correction one to two days, and a full ceramic coating two to three days including curing time. We confirm the exact timeline after inspecting your vehicle.

    Do I need to book in advance?

    Yes. The studio works by appointment only, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Use the booking form below and the team will confirm a slot.

    What does paint correction involve?

    Paint is washed and decontaminated, then machine-polished in stages to remove swirls and light scratches, and finally refined for depth and gloss.

    How do I look after a ceramic coating?

    Wash with a pH-neutral shampoo using the two-bucket method, avoid automatic brush washes, and book a yearly maintenance check to keep the hydrophobic effect strong.
