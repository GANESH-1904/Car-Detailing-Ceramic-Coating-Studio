COLOUR GUIDE

WHAT SUITS YOUR PAINT

Your colour,
our approach.

Pick a colour family for the care we recommend.

Black and dark colours

The most dramatic after correction, and the quickest to show swirls.

  • Multi-stage correction recommended
  • Ceramic coating to resist wash marring
  • Soft drying towels only

White and pearl

Hides swirls well but picks up tar, rust specks and dirt.

  • Iron decontamination at every service
  • Gloss-enhancing coating
  • Gentle polish to protect pearl effect

Red and vivid colours

Strong pigments fade quickly in direct sun without protection.

  • UV-focused ceramic coating
  • Shade parking where possible
  • Yearly inspection for oxidation

Silver and grey

Forgiving on daily drivers, with metallic flake that rewards correction.

  • Single or two-stage correction
  • Hydrophobic coating for easy washing
  • Wheel and glass protection add-on

Questions

Are these photos edited?

No filters or retouching. We use neutral lighting and matched angles so before and after are comparable.

Yes. You receive photo updates at inspection, mid-treatment and hand-over for every booking.

Results depend on paint type and starting condition. Your inspection report sets realistic expectations before we start.
