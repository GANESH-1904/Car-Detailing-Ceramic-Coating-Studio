PAINT DEFECT GUIDE

WHAT IS ON MY PAINT?

Name the problem,
find the fix.

Pick what you see on your car and we will point you to the right treatment.

Swirl marks

Fine circular scratches from automatic washes and dry wiping. Most visible in direct sun.

Best fix: paint correction, then ceramic coating to prevent a repeat.

Water spots

Mineral deposits from hard water that etch into clear coat if left too long.

Best fix: chemical removal and light polish, followed by a hydrophobic coating.

Oxidation and fading

Chalky or dull paint caused by years of UV and heat, common on red and dark colours.

Best fix: multi-stage correction and a UV-resistant coating.

Chips and deeper scratches

Damage through the clear coat cannot be polished out. Light marks can be reduced.

Best fix: assess at inspection. Paint protection film prevents future chips.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Quick
answers.

What is the difference between polishing and ceramic coating?

Polishing removes defects so the paint looks its best. Ceramic coating then locks that finish in with a hard protective layer. Most clients combine them.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes. Combining two treatments saves 5% and three or more save 10% in the estimator, and you only drop the car off once.

Is paint protection film better than ceramic coating?

They do different jobs. Film resists stone chips and scratches. Ceramic gives gloss and easier washing. Many owners choose film on the front and ceramic over the rest.

Will paint correction remove deep scratches?

It removes swirls, haze and light scratches. Scratches that go through the clear coat can be reduced but not fully removed, and we tell you before starting.
