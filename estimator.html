    PRICE QUESTIONS

    GOOD TO KNOW

    Estimate
    FAQs.

    Is the estimate final?

    No. It is a guide based on size and treatments. The final price is confirmed after inspection and put in writing.

    How are vehicle sizes defined?

    Hatchback, sedan, SUV and luxury or sports. If your car sits between two, we quote the nearer size at inspection.

    Do discounts stack?

    Bundle savings are 5% for two treatments and 10% for three or more, applied once to your plan total.

    When do I pay?

    The final price and payment options are confirmed at inspection, before any work begins.
